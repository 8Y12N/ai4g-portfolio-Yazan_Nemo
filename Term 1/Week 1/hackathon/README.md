# The Socratic Stoa

## What it does
The Socratic Stoa is a web app that trains critical thinking through structured philosophical dialogue. Users pick one of 8 essential life questions (e.g. "Is it ever just to lie?", "What is true courage?"), write down their initial answer, then get cross-examined by an AI playing Socrates. Instead of giving lectures, Socrates asks pointed counter-questions over 3-4 rounds to expose gaps or contradictions in the user's thinking. At the end, the app shows a side-by-side comparison of the user's starting position and their refined view.

The app works with or without an API key. Without one, it falls back to a built-in set of pre-written Socratic rebuttals per question, so it never breaks or stalls.

## Who it's for
Students and learners who want to sharpen how they reason about ethics and values, not just what they believe. It's aimed at people already reading or curious about philosophy and comfortable with reflective, text-heavy interaction.

It is not built for younger learners, people who prefer short or casual interactions, or users who are not fluent in English, since the dialogue depends on nuanced language and cultural references to classical philosophy.

## SDG 4: Quality Education
The app addresses a specific gap in how critical thinking is taught: most tools focus on delivering information, not on training how someone questions their own assumptions. The Socratic method forces users to actually defend a position and revise it when it doesn't hold up, which is a different skill than memorizing facts. Anyone using the app walks away having practiced reasoning under pressure, not just having read about it.
